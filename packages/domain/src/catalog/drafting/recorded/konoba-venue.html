OUR STORY

Restaurant Sv. Dominik

At Sv. Dominik we celebrate the rich culinary traditions of the Dalmatian coast, infused with a modern, innovative twist. Each dish we serve tells a story of the land, the sea, and the seasons, crafted from the freshest local ingredients and presented with finesse.

 

Experience the magic of dining under the stars in our beautiful courtyard, where the echoes of centuries-old history meet the flavours of today. Whether you’re joining us for a romantic dinner, a family gathering, a business event, or a special celebration, we’re here to make every moment memorable. Welcome to Sv. Dominik Restaurant – your gateway to an extraordinary culinary journey in Trogir.
