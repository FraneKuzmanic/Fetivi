# How Fetivi orders recommendations

> Draft for the guest Privacy page. Translations come with that page.

When you ask Fetivi for places to go, it orders the options like this:

1. **How well they fit what you asked for**, and what you told us in About me.
2. **How reliable the information is**: places we checked recently come before ones we have not checked in a while.
3. **How close they are** to where you are staying.

## Services you can arrange through Fetivi

Some places offer a service you can arrange right in the chat, like a boat trip or a tour. These places are marked so you can see that you can arrange it there.

They get a small boost, but it only decides between options that fit about equally well. A place that fits your request worse is never put above one that clearly fits better.

The boost is the same for every service you can arrange. It never depends on commission or on any payment to Fetivi, and nobody can pay to be placed higher.
